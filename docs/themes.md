# Themes and contrast

Settings → Appearance offers Dark, Light and Custom themes. The contrast slider (75–300%) adjusts interface text, syntax, line numbers and selections immediately. New profiles and Reset contrast use 300%; saved contrast choices are retained. The sample shows code, selection, search highlighting and status colors before leaving Settings. Show borders toggles the dark outlines of panels and controls independently of contrast.

The standard palettes keep their charcoal and off-white backgrounds. Higher contrast strengthens foregrounds while keeping syntax colors distinct; lower contrast softens them. Borders stay dark at every contrast level. Quick View uses the same theme, contrast and border settings.

Custom colors remain stored exactly as entered. Contrast changes only the displayed palette, so returning to 100% restores the original color values. Switching among themes keeps both the custom color definitions and the contrast preference. Individual colors, or the full custom palette, can still be reset separately.
